Quality at every stage

Real people inspecting real products. Our QA team checks your order from raw material to shipment approval.

  1. 01

    Raw material inspection

    Fabric is checked for defects, shade, weight and width, using the 4-point inspection system.

  2. 02

    Pre-production check

    Approved samples, materials and the production plan are reviewed before cutting.

  3. 03

    Inline inspection

    Checks during sewing catch problems early, while they are easy to fix.

  4. 04

    Final inspection

    Finished goods are inspected using AQL sampling (ISO 2859-1) at the level agreed with you.

  5. 05

    Shipment approval

    Packing, labels, carton marks and quantities are verified before goods leave.

AQL 2.5 / 4.0A common apparel benchmark

Many apparel buyers accept AQL 2.5 for major defects and AQL 4.0 for minor defects. We inspect to the level you set and share a full report before shipment approval.

Certifications our partners hold

Global buyers ask for proof. These are the certifications and programs we look for in the mills and factories we work with.

Showing all 9 certifications

  • GOTS

    GOTS Certified

    Global Organic Textile Standard

    The leading standard for organic textiles. It covers the full chain, from organic fiber to finished garment, with strict environmental and social rules.

    95%Minimum organic fiber for the “organic” label. 70% for “made with organic”.

  • OEKO TEX

    OEKO-TEX STANDARD 100

    Tested for harmful substances

    Every part of the product, from fabric to thread, buttons and zips, is lab tested for harmful substances.

    Class IThe strictest limits, for baby items up to 36 months.

  • GRS

    GRS

    Global Recycled Standard

    Verifies recycled content through the supply chain, with social, environmental and chemical rules at every certified site.

    20%Minimum recycled content to certify. 50% to use the GRS label.

  • SMETA

    Sedex (SMETA Audited)

    Sedex Members Ethical Trade Audit

    One of the most widely used social audits. Results are shared on the Sedex platform, so buyers can review them directly.

    4Pillars: labor, health and safety, environment and business ethics.

  • Higg

    Higg Index

    Higg Facility Environmental Module (FEM)

    Tools created by the Sustainable Apparel Coalition, now Cascale. Factories use the Higg FEM to measure and improve their environmental performance.

    7Impact areas: management, energy, water, wastewater, air, waste and chemicals.

  • Better Work

    Better Work Programme

    A partnership of the ILO and IFC

    Factory assessments, advice and training that improve working conditions and business performance in the garment industry.

    2014The year Better Work Bangladesh began.

  • LEED

    LEED

    Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design

    The U.S. Green Building Council's rating for energy, water, materials and design. Levels run from Certified to Silver, Gold and Platinum.

    280+LEED-certified garment factories in Bangladesh (BGMEA, 2026).

  • RSC

    RSC

    RMG Sustainability Council, Bangladesh

    Bangladesh's national safety body for garment factories. It inspects fire, electrical, structural and boiler safety, and trains workers.

    2020The year RSC took over the Accord's safety work in Bangladesh.

  • ISO

    ISO

    International management system standards

    Certified systems show a factory runs quality, environmental and safety processes in a consistent, audited way.

    • ISO 9001 Quality
    • ISO 14001 Environment
    • ISO 45001 Health and safety

Certifications are held by individual mills and factories. We share valid certificates, audit reports and transaction certificates for the partners making your order.
